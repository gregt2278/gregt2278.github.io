My DAVx5 app

This is a private, non-commercial Google Calendar integration used by its owner on a personally owned Android device.

Purpose

The integration authorizes the open-source DAVx5 application to synchronize one selected Google Calendar through CalDAV into Android's local calendar provider.

Data handling

OAuth tokens and synchronized data are handled locally by DAVx5 on the owner's device. This site operates no application server, receives no calendar or contact data, uses no analytics, and offers no service to third parties.

DAVx5 is an independent open-source project. Learn more at davx5.com.
