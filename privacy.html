Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Scope

My DAVx5 app is a private integration used only by its owner to authorize DAVx5 to synchronize a selected Google Calendar to a personally owned Android device.

Google user data

DAVx5 requests Google Calendar and CardDAV authorization scopes as part of its Google login flow. The owner intends to enable synchronization only for the selected calendar and to leave contact synchronization disabled.

Use and storage

Authorization tokens and synchronized calendar data are stored locally by DAVx5 on the owner's Android device. The operator of this informational site does not receive, transmit, copy, sell, or share Google user data. This site contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, forms, databases, or application backend.

Retention and revocation

The owner can remove the DAVx5 account or uninstall DAVx5 to delete locally stored authorization and synchronization data. Google access can also be revoked from the Google Account permissions page.

Contact

Questions can be submitted through the owner's GitHub profile.
